Popular Trekking Destinations

Himalayan Trek

Region: Northern India

Difficulty: Moderate to Difficult

Experience spectacular mountain views and peaceful trails.

Western Ghats

Region: Western India

Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Explore green hills, waterfalls and rich biodiversity.

Araku Valley

Region: Andhra Pradesh

Difficulty: Easy

Enjoy scenic valleys, coffee plantations and tribal culture.

Other Attractions by Type

  • Historical Monuments: Taj Mahal, Red Fort and Hampi.
  • Nature: Western Ghats, Araku Valley and Himalayan landscapes.
  • Beaches: Goa and Visakhapatnam.
  • Cultural Heritage: Jaipur, Varanasi and Madurai.

Travel Tips

  • Check the weather before starting a trek.
  • Wear suitable shoes and comfortable clothing.
  • Carry drinking water and basic first-aid supplies.
  • Follow local rules and avoid littering.
