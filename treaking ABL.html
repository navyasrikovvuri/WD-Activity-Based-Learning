Prepare for Your Adventure

Pack smart and follow safety guidelines before every trip.

🎒 Equipment Checklist

  • Comfortable trekking shoes
  • Backpack and water bottle
  • First-aid kit and personal medicines
  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Raincoat or waterproof jacket
  • Flashlight and extra batteries
  • Energy snacks and packed food
  • Map, compass, or charged GPS device
  • Sleeping bag for camping trips
  • Sun protection and insect repellent

⚠️ Safety Guidelines

  • Follow your trek leader's instructions.
  • Stay with your trekking group.
  • Check weather conditions before departure.
  • Carry enough drinking water.
  • Do not litter or disturb wildlife.
  • Avoid unsafe shortcuts and restricted areas.
  • Inform the guide about medical conditions.
  • Carry emergency contact information.
  • Turn back if conditions become unsafe.
  • Choose a route suited to your fitness level.
